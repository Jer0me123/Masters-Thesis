\chapter{Methodology}

\section{Image Retrieval and Dataset Construction}

Re-LAION-2B-en-research-safe was processed across four randomly selected shards,
yielding 65,552,809 URL-caption pairs of which 43,491,938 images were successfully
retrieved (link decay rate 33.7\%). CLIP ViT-L/14 embeddings were computed, L2-normalised,
and indexed per shard using FAISS IndexFlatIP for exact cosine similarity retrieval. A
set of 200 ISCO-08-aligned occupations was retrieved using the template
\texttt{"A photo of a/an \{profession\}"} with gender-neutral phrasing, filtered via a
negative prompt dominance criterion, and passed through a multi-stage structural
validation pipeline requiring exactly one detected face, verified face-person spatial
association, and successful MediaPipe FaceMesh landmark extraction, with images retained
even where person detection was imperfect provided the face and FaceMesh criteria were satisfied.
YOLOv12l-Face was selected as face detector (AP@0.5 0.905, highest small-face recall of four candidates).
The top 1,000 structurally valid images per occupation were retained, yielding 200,000 images at a mean acceptance rate of 33.51\%.
A corresponding 200,000-image generation corpus was produced using SDv1.5 with LCM-LoRA
acceleration, selected for throughput (1.5\,s/image versus 5.6\,s for standard SDv1.5)
from three LAION-traceable configurations. A full-body profession-conditioned prompt
with neutral professional framing was used throughout; classifier-free guidance was fixed
at $s{=}1.0$ to establish a conservative lower bound on bias free from inference-time
amplification. Generated images passed through an analogous structural validation
pipeline, achieving a mean acceptance rate of 66.38\%.



\section{Demographic Annotation}

All images underwent FaceMesh face segmentation producing background-removed crops to
prevent background shortcut learning. Gender was annotated using the Realistic Gender
Classifier, selected from five candidates for its highest CCv2 face-only accuracy
(88.6\%) and smallest male-female gap among candidates with comparable overall accuracy
(6.2\,pp; the only candidate with a smaller gap sacrificed 11.5\,pp overall accuracy).
Skin tone was estimated using a VGG-16 MST10 regression model selected via factorial
ablation across output formulation, colour space, label granularity, and background
treatment, with predictions collapsed into Light (MST~1--3), Mid (MST~4--7), and Dark
(MST~8--10) bins. All labels are treated as perceived-attribute proxies. Distributions
were contextualised against BLS and ILOSTAT reference statistics; a race-to-skin-tone
mapping via FairFace was abandoned due to mid-bin collapse.

\section{Image Transformation Pipeline}

The pipeline serves two objectives: dataset classification (ConvNeXt-Tiny distinguishing
Re-LAION-5B, SDv1.5, and COCO per transformation) and demographic artefact probing
(predicting gender and skin tone from isolated visual channels). Adding COCO as a third
class enables per-class accuracy attribution, localising which dataset carries each
spurious signal. Demographic probes use deliberately simple models: logistic regression
for low-dimensional inputs and ResNet-50 for spatial inputs, following Meister et al.

Transformations span three levels. \textit{Structural:} monocular depth (Depth-Anything-V2
ViT-S), Canny edge detection, low-pass and high-pass frequency filtering (hard circular
FFT mask, $r{=}40$), and YOLOv8l-Pose keypoints (34-dimensional normalised vector).
\textit{Spatial-statistical:} pixel shuffle, patch shuffle at four scales
($2{\times}2$--$16{\times}16$), and mean RGB. \textit{Semantic:} Mask2Former
segmentation (Swin-Large, ADE20K), YOLOv8x object detection (OpenImages V7, demographic
categories remapped), BLIP Large captions (gender-neutral remapped), and SDv1.5 VAE
reconstruction. Occlusion variants follow Meister et al.'s five-condition protocol: Full
NoBg, MaskSegm, MaskSegm NoBg, MaskRect, and MaskRect NoBg, with person masks from
Mask2Former COCO (96.5\% detection rate, highest of five candidates).

\section{Inference-Time Debiasing Pipeline}

A 10,000-image debiased corpus was generated for the doctor profession at $s{=}7.0$,
fully activating the model's demographic prior as a demanding test of the debiasing
interventions. ITI-GEN~\cite{Zhang_2023_ITIGEN} inclusive tokens were trained on CCv2
across gender and skin tone (MST~1--10), producing 20 demographic combinations of 500
images each, applied via the \texttt{prompt\_prepend} mechanism. ControlNet
OpenPose~\cite{LZhang_2023_ControlNet} (scale 0.6, fixed canonical skeleton) enforced
structural consistency and reduced pose-correlated annotation confounds.
Chamfer-based colour alignment~\cite{Shum_2025_ColorAlignment} steered the predicted
clean sample $\hat{x}_0$ at timesteps $t{>}600$ toward a uniform mean-RGB reference
derived from the standard SDv1.5 doctor corpus. LCM-LoRA was excluded due to incoherent
outputs under per-step Chamfer modification; SW-Guidance was excluded on throughput
grounds.
